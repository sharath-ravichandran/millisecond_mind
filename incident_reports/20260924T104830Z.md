## FastPay Outage and Traffic Reroute

- **SecurePay:**

### Baseline Comparison
- **FastPay Baseline (same hour of day, last 30 days):**
  - Average Failure Rate: 0.007764 (0.7764%)
  - Total Transactions (30 days): 998710
- The live error rate for FastPay (25.6%) is approximately 33 times higher than its baseline (0.7764%), with a large sample size of 6351 transactions.

### SQL Used for Baseline
```sql
SELECT
  AVG(SAFE_DIVIDE(failed_txns, total_txns)) AS baseline_failure_rate,
  SUM(total_txns) AS total_txns_30_days
FROM
  `project-df4ea4c6-ceb3-42b7-970.checkout_ops.gateway_hourly_stats`
WHERE
  gateway = 'FastPay'
  AND hour_of_day = 10
  AND hour_ts >= TIMESTAMP('2026-08-25 00:00:00 UTC')
  AND hour_ts < TIMESTAMP('2026-09-24 00:00:00 UTC')
```

### Action Taken
- Traffic has been rerouted from **FastPay** to **SecurePay**.
- **Reason:** Live error rate for FastPay (25.6%) is 33x higher than baseline (0.7764%) with a large sample size (6351). SecurePay is healthy with an error rate of 0.71%.
- The reroute is effective immediately and will expire in 30 minutes.

- Investigate the root cause of the elevated error rate on FastPay.
