### Baseline Comparison:
The baseline failure rate for FastPay for hour_of_day 12 over the last 30 days was calculated using the following SQL query:

```sql
SELECT
  AVG(SAFE_DIVIDE(failed_txns, total_txns)) AS baseline_failure_rate,
  SUM(total_txns) AS total_txns_in_baseline
FROM
  `project-df4ea4c6-ceb3-42b7-970.checkout_ops.gateway_hourly_stats`
WHERE
  gateway = 'FastPay'
  AND hour_of_day = 12
  AND hour_ts BETWEEN TIMESTAMP_SUB(TIMESTAMP('2026-09-24T12:00:00Z'), INTERVAL 30 DAY) AND TIMESTAMP('2026-09-24T12:00:00Z')
```

*   **Baseline Failure Rate (FastPay):** 0.79%
*   **Total Transactions in Baseline:** 853451
*   **Comparison:** The live error rate of FastPay (24.3%) is approximately 30.68 times higher than its historical baseline (0.79%) for the same hour of day, with a significant sample size of 2893 transactions.

### Action Taken:
Traffic has been rerouted from **FastPay** to **SecurePay**.
**Reason:** FastPay's live error rate (24.3%) is 30x higher than its baseline (0.79%) with a large sample size (2893 transactions). SecurePay was identified as a healthy alternative with a live error rate of 0.57%.

### Next Steps:
Monitor FastPay's error rate and investigate the root cause of the 504_TIMEOUT errors. Consider reverting traffic once FastPay's performance returns to normal.
